SoftSync AI provides an all‑in‑one CRM platform enhanced with AI assistants that automate lead capture, email outreach, meeting notes, and workflow automation. Targeted at startups and small teams, it offers tiered pricing, API access, and integrations with Claude or ChatGPT via MCP, delivering a unified business software experience.

**Areas:** Company · CRM · AI · SaaS · BusinessSoftware
